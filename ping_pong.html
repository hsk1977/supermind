Table Tennis

You are red, the robot is blue.
Move the mouse / drag your finger (or use arrow keys) to move your paddle. It hits automatically once the ball has bounced on your side. Where the ball meets your paddle sets the direction. A red ring shows where the ball can be returned, and your paddle drifts toward it. Hold Shift for a power smash.
Click, tap or press Space to serve. The ball must clear the net and bounce once on your opponent's half.
